Know what’s due. Know when to start.

Carmen Planner is a free desktop app for Ohio State students. Sign in to Carmen, and it turns every course into one list of what’s due this week and a study plan that fits your evenings.

Sample courses. In the app, this comes from your own Carmen.

Five courses, one plan.

No more clicking through every course on Sunday night. Carmen Planner reads them all at once and lays out your week.

Due this week

Every assignment, quiz and exam due this week, grouped by day. Overdue work goes on top. Check things off as you finish.

A study plan that fits

Tell it how many hours you have and when you like to start. It spreads the work across the days before each deadline.

Two-week heads-up

Midterms and big projects show up two weeks out, with the exact day to start, so exam prep stops happening the night before.

An optional weekly brief from Claude.

The plan is made on your computer for free and works without AI. If you add your own Claude API key in Settings, Claude also reads your due dates and syllabi and writes a short brief for the week.

It tells you what to do first, gives a note for each course, and points out crunch points, like two exams on back-to-back days.

A front-loaded week: two exams and an essay draft land close together.
  • Pre-Lab 5. Due tonight and needed for tomorrow’s lab.
  • PSYCH Exam 2. A practice set today shows which chapters need time.
  • Watch out: PSYCH Exam 2 and MATH Midterm 2 are one day apart.

Small sessions starting today beat one huge session on Sunday.

Your Carmen stays yours.

You sign in yourselfThe sign-in happens on Ohio State’s own page, with Duo, inside the app. The app never sees your password.
Read onlyIt reads your courses and due dates. It never submits, edits or posts anything.
Stays on your computerYour course data is saved only on your computer. There is no account and no server.
Remove it anytime“Sign out and clear data” in Settings deletes your sign-in and everything the app saved.

Download Carmen Planner

Free. Version 1.0. Want to look around first? Open the app and choose “Try it with sample courses”.

Mac

Download .dmg

macOS 11 or newer. Apple silicon and Intel.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Carmen Planner into Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. This is needed because the app is not from the App Store.
  3. No Open button? Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr "/Applications/Carmen Planner.app" in Terminal.

Windows

Download .exe

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

  1. Open the downloaded file to start the installer.
  2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway. This is because the app is not signed by a paid certificate.
  3. Follow the installer. Carmen Planner appears on your desktop and in the Start menu.

How I built it with AI

Made for the Ohio State “Build what you want” challenge in October 2026, with Claude as my coding partner.

From a website to an app

I first pictured a website, but a website can’t sign in to Carmen for you. So it became a desktop app with its own Carmen sign-in window.

Fixing the plan

The first plan crammed exam prep into the night before, because small homework filled the early days. Now exams and projects get time reserved first.

Testing with screenshots

Claude ran the app, looked at screenshots, and fixed what looked wrong, like cut-off names and overcrowded days.
